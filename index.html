ShrinkRay

Your cereal box didn't get cheaper - it got smaller. ShrinkRay tracks package sizes against prices and calls out every hidden hike.

Point the ray

Log what you actually paid and how much was in the package, each time you buy. ShrinkRay computes the price per 100 grams (or ml, or count) across every purchase and flags the exact moment a product shrank while its price "stayed the same". Includes a quick two-pack comparator for the supermarket shelf. Everything stays in your browser.
